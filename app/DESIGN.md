# Homepage design — personal research portfolio

## Approved direction · 2026-11-08

The approved Compute-inspired portfolio uses: a charcoal canvas, expansive left-aligned introduction, fine grid, white pill actions and luminous scenery that belongs to the background. The current default is a downloaded volumetric flame rendered as a cobalt/lilac luminous plume at right. An official Three Water alternative is available through `background=water`. Preserve Nim's concise structure, Magic Portfolio's substantial work presentation, actual Motion Primitives interactions, English/Chinese, authentic covers, organization marks, account identities and the three research areas. This is the approved flame edition.

Nim and Magic Portfolio are visual references. Their template code was not copied. Motion Primitives Morphing Dialog was adapted from its MIT core; upstream source, source hashes, changes and copyright notice are retained in `third-party/motion-primitives/` and `public/licenses/`.

## Composition

A 1240px maximum shell with 48px desktop side padding: quiet monogram/navigation, an expansive personal introduction, selected work with research-area navigation, a featured publication, three compact resource rows, industry/academic history, and a small footer. A faint 128px grid, page-edge guides and a decorative cross establish one alignment system. On phones the featured paper stacks, resource rows retain their thumbnail, and career dates sit beneath identities. Official institution marks retain their colors on small white plates.

The personal name is the primary heading, preceded by the research-engineer role. The introduction states the current work at AMEC. Current account @phoenixsjtu and earlier work @phoenixdyf are visible. Housz ThreeVolumetricFire supplies a large curved luminous volume with original noise/profile textures, graded cobalt/lilac/white, plus sparse embers and gentle camera parallax. The optional water background uses official Three Water with fine normal-map motion, optical reflections and off-camera light panels. Both sit behind the whole page, with a dark reading shade at left. They are decorative material studies rather than measured simulations or standalone animation cards.

## Typography and palette

Self-hosted official Geist Sans variable, with SF/Helvetica/PingFang fallbacks for Chinese. Name 40–88px, section 26–32px, featured title 26–32px, resource title 16–20px, primary body 16px, secondary information 13–14px. Lighter display weights and generous line-height create the reference's open composition. Stable text measures and deliberate title wrapping in both languages. The font choice intentionally follows the user's Vercel reference.

Charcoal #080a0f, primary text #f2f4fa, secondary #a7aebd, readable pale cobalt #a5bdff, translucent fine dividers, and navy publication media. No green. A precise 8px featured-paper frame, fine media grid, subtle depth beneath the authentic paper, flat resource rows, and pill controls establish a quiet cinematic character. Paper images remain unmodified white documents. The native modal is dark with white paper media and a high-contrast close control.

## Work and sources

TPTLN leads with its complete original publication first page next to the project description, authors and Paper/Code links. Remaining projects use attributed repository document excerpts. All four stay under predictive maintenance; physics + AI and digital twins show their methods and forthcoming status without fake project links. Images use BASE_URL for static-host path compatibility.

## Interaction

Actual Motion Primitives Morphing Dialog expands the cover into an image-and-context reading view, with publication citation, DOI/source and code links. Native modal semantics provide background inertness and focus containment. Close, Escape and backdrop dismissal return focus to the original trigger. The reader can scroll through a tall document on a narrow screen; body scrolling is locked while open. Reduced motion removes shared layout movement. Existing Cult UI direction-aware research tabs retain keyboard semantics and the moving active indicator; TextureCard remains with flattened decorative layers.

One shared Three renderer wrapper owns both scenes' lifecycle. Motion control pauses frame scheduling; hidden pages pause; reduced-motion users start with a still frame and may explicitly resume. Render budgets are 650,000 pixels on desktop and 200,000 on phones, at most30fps. Static fallback handles load/context failures. The original Housz source and assets are pinned and unchanged; instance shader grading and scene composition live in the local factory. Official Three Water is imported from the existing0.187 package. The new source/texture ZIP includes notices and provenance. Geist is self-hosted with original notices. Vanta, Paper Shaders, FluidScene and EngineeringScene remain as inactive earlier source.
